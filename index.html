04 / MENTORING & PEOPLE DEVELOPMENT
01

Scholarship Mentor

SASC (Student Advisory & Support Center) is a division at BINUS that provides counseling services for Binusians. SASC also runs a scholarship mentor program for Binusians who meet the eligibility requirements to become mentors. In my third semester, I was accepted as a scholarship mentor. A mentor’s responsibilities include monitoring the mentee’s progress, serving as a support system for the mentee, and teaching the mentee course material related to their studies. Mentors are required to meet with their mentees at least 10 times per semester, either in person or online. It’s a valuable experience to be trusted by SASC to guide a mentee.

Freshmen Leader

At the start of the third semester, I applied to become a Freshmen Leader (FL) for Binusian 2029. And it turns out I was accepted. The duties of an FL include welcoming freshmen, introducing them to campus life at BINUS, and explaining graduation requirements to all BINUS students. I served from August 18th – 27th. I was very happy to be able to share the experiences and knowledge I’ve gained over the past year.

NOV 2024

FoodCycle Indonesia

On 22nd November 2024, I had the opportunity to volunteer with the Foodcycle Indonesia community located in Matraman, East Jakarta. FoodCycle is a non-profit organization that acts as a food bank to store food waste, process it, and distribute it to marginalized communities. In this volunteer activity, we are in charge of sorting rejected factory bread. Foodcycle Indonesia can sort 100 kg of bread a day. The bread is factory reject bread that is still edible but no longer suitable for sale. The bread is not just distributed; it is given to orphanages, nursing homes, etc., so that it is not misdirected. This activity reminds me of the importance of finishing our food. We also had the opportunity to interview Kak Kukuh as one of the organizers of Foodcycle Indonesia. It was a fun and new experience for me.
